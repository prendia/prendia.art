Academic Archive

My background sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and complex biological systems. I'm particularly drawn to evolutionary computation: the idea that intelligence and behaviour can emerge from simple rules over time.

My work includes genetic simulations, fractal modelling, computational biology, agent-based systems and applied machine learning.

On the applied side, I've been involved in group projects such as creating an amphibious robot. Individual projects include using eye-tracking for attention-awareness system using support vector machines (SVMs).
