About

Hello fellow internet person. My name is Prendia. I'm fascinated by the threads that connect ideas, people and the world. I started out exploring maths, physics and computer science, wanting to understand the world through algorithms and logic. I tried to fit the whole world into a little deterministic box. Over time, I realised that life tends not to play that way and instead found the most interesting questions sit in the relationships between people... In art, books, poetry, music... Here, I am looking to share bits of this thread. It's a space to explore, make (some) sense and hopefully connect with others along the way.
