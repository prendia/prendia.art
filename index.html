About

Technology can be really cool. A friend said to me recently, "imagine being able to tell someone living 50 years ago that we can listen to The Beatles anywhere, anytime using an electric brick in our pockets, they'd go mental." He was right, it would absolutely knock their socks off.

Not only can we listen to The Beatles on the bus if our heart so desires, but there is also everything else that technology can enble for us on Earth.

Technology can be really cool and somehow we've found ourselves missing the mark. More than that, I think it is costing us something. We're staring at screens while the things that have connected humans across centuries ~visual arts, music, storytelling~ are losing their audience. I can't change the direction the industry is heading, but I can use what I know to build a stronger presence for the things that connect us.

This is my small ripple in the water to push back and give art an online presence.
